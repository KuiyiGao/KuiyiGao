# Kuiyi (Cory) Gao

I'm an undergraduate at UNC-Chapel Hill, expecting to graduate in May 2027. I work on **AI safety and auditable AI deployment**. I have done research with Tencent AI Lab, at CUHK, and through MBZUAI UGRIP. I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

[Homepage](https://kuiyigao.github.io) · [CV](https://kuiyigao.github.io/Kuiyi_Gao_CV.pdf) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Yobg_TQAAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:kuiyigao@unc.edu)

I want to understand how AI systems handle unsafe inputs, changing environments, and conflicting human values. My interests include safety evaluation and runtime checks, with an emphasis on keeping people able to inspect and control agent actions.

### Research

- **MMSafeAware — ACL 2025, third author.** Image–text safety judgments, including missed risks and false alarms. My work included benchmark construction, evaluation methods, vision-centric fine-tuning, and rebuttal. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.832/) · [Code](https://github.com/Jarviswang94/MMSafetyAwareness)
- **Chain-of-Jailbreak — Findings of ACL 2025, second author.** Safety in multi-turn image editing. I designed the method and worked on the defense. I contributed benchmark data, ran most experiments, and worked on figures and writing. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.571/) · [Code](https://github.com/Jarviswang94/Chain-of-Jailbreak)
- **RealGUINoise — co-first author, under review.** Agent behavior on original and altered web and Android interfaces. My work covers dataset construction, evaluation methodology, automated judging, and experiments.
- **RuntimeTickets — first author, under review at ICLR 2027.** Runtime checks and records of the evidence behind safety decisions. I built the UGRIP team's runtime monitor and lead the follow-up Ticket study. [System code](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/OpenClaw-Skill-Hack)

### Projects

- [MemSlot](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/Distorted-OCR-Compression): attention over frozen RoBERTa features to guide contract text selection and page layout for OCR. Full-pipeline QA evaluation remains incomplete.
- [Movability segmentation](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/QuickMotionDetection): DeepLabV3 with a custom ASPP head and four classes derived from COCO-Stuff.
- [Poetry and image variation](https://kuiyigao.github.io/#poetry): analysis of saved CLIP-based differences among images generated from the same poem excerpt.
- [Text-KEN](https://kuiyigao.github.io/#text-ken): a numerical prototype comparing sentence embeddings with RBF kernels and spectral entropy.
