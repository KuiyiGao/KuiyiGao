# Kuiyi (Cory) Gao

I'm an undergraduate at UNC-Chapel Hill. I study the safety of models that work with images and text, and the reliability of agents that use web and mobile interfaces. Previously, I studied at CUHK and worked with Tencent AI Lab. I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

[Homepage](https://kuiyigao.github.io) · [CV](https://kuiyigao.github.io/Kuiyi_Gao_CV.pdf) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Yobg_TQAAAAJ) · [Email](mailto:kuiyigao@unc.edu)

### Research

- **MMSafeAware:** testing whether models correctly judge image–text pairs as safe or unsafe. ACL 2025. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.832/) · [Code](https://github.com/Jarviswang94/MMSafetyAwareness)
- **Chain-of-Jailbreak:** evaluating unsafe image generation during multi-turn editing. Findings of ACL 2025. [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.571/) · [Code](https://github.com/Jarviswang94/Chain-of-Jailbreak)
- **RealGUINoise:** testing agents on the same web and Android tasks with original and altered interfaces. Co-first-authored manuscript under review.
- **Runtime safety for agent skills:** monitoring skill activity, checking permissions, and recording why actions are allowed or blocked. Ongoing research. [Code](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/OpenClaw-Skill-Hack)

### Projects

- [MemSlot](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/Distorted-OCR-Compression): scoring words in contracts to guide text selection and page layout for OCR. Full-pipeline question-answering evaluation is not yet complete.
- [Movability segmentation](https://github.com/KuiyiGao/QuickMotionDetection): DeepLabV3 with a custom ASPP head, grouping COCO-Stuff labels into four movability classes.
- [Poetry and image variation](https://kuiyigao.github.io/#poetry): analyzing saved CLIP-based scores of differences among images generated from the same poem excerpt.
- [Text-KEN](https://kuiyigao.github.io/#text-ken): a prototype comparing two sets of sentence embeddings using RBF kernels and spectral entropy.
